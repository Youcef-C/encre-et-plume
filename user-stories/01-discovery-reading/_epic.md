# Epic 01 — Discovery & Reading

## Goal

Give every visitor and member of "Encre & Plume" a compelling way to find manga, romans, and illustrations and to read them comfortably. This epic covers the public showroom landing ("Accueil"), the faceted catalog ("Découvrir"), individual work pages ("Œuvre"), the immersive chapter reader ("Lecteur"), the illustration gallery ("Galerie") and illustration detail, the all-time ranking ("Classement"), the personal watchlist and favorites view ("Ma liste & coups de cœur"), and the cross-cutting like/favorite mechanism that ties them together. Together these screens form the top of the funnel: they let visitors browse and sample content, let readers track and resume what they love, and surface trending works and creators that feed engagement, monetization, and collaboration elsewhere in the product.

## Personas

- **Visitor** — unauthenticated; can browse and read public content, prompted to sign in for personal actions.
- **Reader (lecteur·rice)** — authenticated consumer; reads, likes, saves, tracks progress.
- **Creator / Writer (scénariste) / Illustrator (dessinateur·rice)** — surfaced as "Top artiste/scénariste" and team members; followable and supportable from discovery surfaces.
- **Publisher/Editor** — owns "SÉLECTION ÉDITEUR" and editor picks shown in the catalog rail.
- **Admin** — sees moderation bars embedded in work and illustration detail pages.
