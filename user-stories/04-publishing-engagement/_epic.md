# Epic 04 — Publishing & Engagement

This epic covers the moment a work goes live and everything readers do around it. Creators publish (or schedule) chapters and flip them between free and premium; readers comment, leave structured reviews ("Avis"), follow creators, share, and report problematic content; and the platform surfaces contests and a news feed that drive participation. It is the bridge between the private Creation Studio (epic 03) and the public Discovery & Reading surfaces (epic 01), and it feeds notifications (F-5) and moderation (epic 07).

## Personas
- **Creator** (writer / illustrator / project owner) — publishes chapters, sets access, follows other creators, responds to and moderates comments on own works.
- **Reader** (lecteur·rice) — reads, comments, leaves reviews, follows, shares, reports, enters contests.
- **Publisher/Editor** — creates and runs contests ([[PE-6]]).
- **Admin** — moderates comments/reviews/reports, administers contests, authors news articles.
- **Visitor** — can view shared public content without an account.
